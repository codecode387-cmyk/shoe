The Florentine Heritage

A Century Of Knowing Which Shoes Are Worth Selling

Founded along the banks of the Arno in 1928, Emmalex remains a family-run house, not a factory. We travel to the ateliers of Italy, France, and England, and only bring home pairs built on hand-hammered cork footbeds and oak-tanned hides — never composite fillers.

public

French Tannery Provenance

We only carry pairs built from Grade-1 full-grain skins tanned in Le Puy-en-Velay, chosen for suppleness and grain clarity.

palette

Hand-Applied Patina

Every pair we stock is finished layer-by-layer by its maker with natural pigments and carnauba wax, for a depth no factory dye matches.

construction

Goodyear & Blake Rapid

We favor makers who still sew with waxed Irish linen thread — construction that can be rebuilt indefinitely and molds to your gait.

build

Lifetime Restoration

Bring any pair bought from us back to our Florence counter, any year. We coordinate the strip, re-welt, and leather nourishment.
